What to expect

A haircut is a shape around the face. We start with a short talk about your face, your hair and how you style it, then cut and finish it the way you choose: a natural quick dry, a quick brushed styling, or a full blow-out, bouncy or sleek, that usually lasts 2–3 days.

For colour we use L’Oréal Professionnel and Redken, including ammonia-free options and bleach-free lightening with L’Oréal Blond Studio No Bleach™. Every price includes a set amount of product; anything extra is agreed before we start.

Questions

Are you open on Mondays or in the evening?

Usually yes. We work on Mondays and until late on Wednesday and Saturday afternoons, when many salons in Athens are closed or close early. You see live free slots in online booking; our current opening hours are always on Google.

Do you speak English?

Yes. Book online in English, or message us on WhatsApp or Viber at +30 698 131 9000.

Where is the salon?

Erisichthonos 3-5, Thiseio, central Athens: about 5 minutes on foot from Thiseio metro station, a short walk from the Acropolis area and Monastiraki.

How do I pay?

Online when you book, or at the salon after your appointment. Prices are the same online and at the salon.

Can I just walk in?

If there is a free chair, yes, but booking online is the safest way to get the time you want, especially when you are in Athens for a few days.
